          ➕ Apna Gaana Jodo

          Hamare Baare Mein

          Rangeela Salon Radio · Film Magazine Issue #90

          Kabhi kisi chhote shehar ki gali mein gaye hain? Jahan mint-green deewar, bada mirror aur uske 10 bulbs, ceiling fan ki ahista goonj, aur counter pe ek purana transistor radio. Rangeela Salon Radio wahi mahaul hai — Pappu Bhai ka salon, jahan hair cut ke saath 90s ke Hindi gaane free mein chalte hain.

          Yahan 1990 se 2009 tak ke gaane bajte hain — woh zamana jab cassette hoti thi, radio pe farmaishi hoti thi, aur har gaana poora suna jaata tha. Moods chuno: Baarish, Romance, Masti, Dard Bhare, Road Trip ya Chai Time. Farmaish bhejo, dedication bhejo — purane Akashvani style mein.

          Ek baat saaf: hum koi gaana apne paas rakhte ya host nahi karte. Har gaana official YouTube upload hai, YouTube ke apne player mein CRT TV par chalta hai. Hum sirf salon ka mahaul, radio ke controls aur bhaiya ki baatein banate hain.

          "Baal to wapas aa jaate hain, gaane wapas nahi aate — isliye hum dono sambhal ke sunein."
          — Pappu Bhai, Prop.

          Sawaal-Jawaab (FAQ)

          Index cards ki tarah — ek kholo, ek sawaal ka jawab milo.

          Kya account banana padega?

          Nahi! Na login, na app, na payment. Browser kholo, power knob dabao, gaane shuru.

          Gaane kahan se aate hain?

          Sabhi gaane official YouTube videos hain jo YouTube ke apne embedded player mein chalte hain — salon ke CRT TV par. Hum khud koi music download, host ya proxy nahi karte.

          Bhaiya kaun hai?

          Pappu Bhai — salon ke maalik aur radio wale. Jab AI service available ho, unki Hinglish lines AI (GLM) likhta hai; warna hamari apni likhi built-in lines chalti hain. Yaad rakho: AI-written hone par bhi yeh sirf mazaak-mizaj ke lines hain, koi asli insaan nahi bol raha.

          Kya mobile pe chalega?

          Haan, browser mein hi chalega. Pehle tap zaroori hai (browser rule hai), uske baad gaane chalte rahenge. Ek limit: phone lock hone par gaana ruk sakta hai — platform ki limit hai, humari nahi.

          Kuch gaane skip kyun ho jaate hain?

          Kuch video owners embedding allow nahi karte, ya video hat jaata hai. Aise gaane radio khud skip kar deti hai — "Yeh gaana abhi nahi chal sakta, agla…" bol kar. Aap kuch mat karo, bhaiya sambhal lenge.

          Mera data kahan jaata hai?

          Aapke pasandida gaane, diary aur settings sirf aapke browser (localStorage) mein rehte hain — kahin bheje nahi jaate. Jab AI DJ on ho, to gaane ka naam aur dedication ke naam AI service ko line likhne ke liye jaate hain. Bas.

          Gaana kaise suggest karein?

          Diary section mein "Apna Gaana Jodo" se YouTube link paste karo — video check hoke playlist mein jud jaayega. Ya Farmaish mein bhaiya se mango!
